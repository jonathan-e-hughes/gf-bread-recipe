No-Fuss Gluten-Free Dutch Oven Bread

A reliable gluten-free loaf built around King Arthur Measure for Measure flour, psyllium husk, and a small amount of buckwheat or sorghum flour.

Jump to recipe

Why this recipe works

Finding a good gluten-free bread recipe can be frustrating. Many recipes ask for a laborious specialty flour blend, a long list of hard-to-find ingredients, or a fussy process that makes the whole project feel more like a chemistry experiment than a loaf of bread.

This version is intentionally no-fuss. It uses King Arthur Measure for Measure flour as the base, then adds only buckwheat flour or sorghum flour for better flavor and structure. Psyllium husk gives the dough the stretch and body that gluten-free bread usually lacks, while the Dutch oven helps create steam for a better crust.

The result is a rustic, practical loaf: simple ingredients, a manageable process, and a gluten-free bread that actually feels worth making again.

GF Bread Recipe

  • 8g active dry yeast
  • 20g granulated sugar
  • 405g warm water
  • 20g psyllium husk
  • 65g buckwheat flour OR 65g sorghum flour
  • 255g King Arthur measure for measure
  • 10g sea salt
  • 12 apple cider vinegar

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, mix together the yeast, sugar and 150 g (1/2 cup + 2 tbsp) warm water. Set aside for 5 – 10 minutes, or until the mixture starts frothing.
  2. In a separate bowl, mix together the psyllium husk and 240 g (1 cup) water. After about 15 – 30 seconds, a gel will form.
  3. In a large bowl, mix together the buckwheat/sorghum flour, measure for measure flour, and salt, until evenly combined.
  4. Add the yeast mixture, psyllium gel and apple cider vinegar to the dry ingredients. Knead the dough by hand until smooth and it starts coming away from the bowl, about 1-2 minutes.
  5. Forming the dough into a smooth ball. Cover with a damp tea towel and allow to rise in a warm place for about 3 hours or until doubled in size.
  6. During the last 30min. of the rise, pre-heat the oven to 480 ºF (250 ºC) Dutch oven on the lower middle rack.
  7. Remove the Dutch oven. Sprinkle a tablespoon of corn meal on the bottom of the Dutch oven. Add the dough, sprinkle with GF flour, and score the top with a pattern of choice (the easiest pattern is a cross, about ¼ – ½ inch deep) using a sharp knife.
  8. Cover and bake (with steam) at 480 ºF (250 ºC) for 30 minutes – don’t open the Dutch oven during this initial period, as that would allow the steam to.
  9. After the 30 minutes, uncover the Dutch oven, reduce the oven temperature to 450 ºF (230 ºC), and bake for a further 35 minutes.
  10. Transfer the loaf onto a wire cooling rack to cool completely.
