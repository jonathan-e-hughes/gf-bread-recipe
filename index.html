Foolproof Gluten-Free Dutch Oven Bread

A reliable gluten-free loaf built around King Arthur Measure for Measure flour, psyllium husk, and a small amount of buckwheat or sorghum flour.

Jump to recipe

Why this recipe works

Finding a good gluten-free bread recipe can be frustrating. Many recipes ask for a laborious specialty flour blend or a long list of hard-to-find ingredients.

This version is intentionally simple. It uses King Arthur Measure for Measure flour as the base, then adds only buckwheat or sorghum flour. Psyllium husk gives the dough structure, while the Dutch oven creates steam for a proper crust.

The result is a practical, repeatable gluten-free loaf that actually works.

GF Bread Recipe

  • 8g active dry yeast
  • 20g granulated sugar
  • 405g warm water
  • 20g psyllium husk
  • 65g buckwheat flour OR 65g sorghum flour
  • 255g King Arthur measure for measure
  • 10g sea salt
  • 12 apple cider vinegar

Instructions

  1. Mix yeast, sugar, and 150g warm water. Let sit 5–10 minutes.
  2. Mix psyllium husk with 240g water until gel forms.
  3. Combine flours and salt.
  4. Add yeast mixture, psyllium gel, and vinegar. Knead briefly.
  5. Let rise ~3 hours until doubled.
  6. Preheat Dutch oven to 480°F during last 30 minutes.
  7. Transfer dough, score top.
  8. Bake covered 30 minutes.
  9. Uncover, reduce heat to 450°F, bake 35 minutes more.
  10. Cool completely.
